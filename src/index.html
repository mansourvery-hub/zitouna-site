How this demo differs from the app
  • It shows the parcel screen only. The app's welcome screen, My Trees list, Harvest and Years tabs, settings, backup and map are not included.
  • The ripeness rules (weights, rounding, Chemlali thresholds) and the rendement formula are the app's. The gauge geometry and the theme colours follow the app's code, in the light or dark theme your device uses.
  • Nothing is stored. Refresh the page and your checks are gone.
  • The app is still changing, so layout and wording here may differ from the current release.
  • Not reproduced: the gauge animation, the olive-branch artwork inside the gauge, the camera hint, the app's exact date format, and the app's Mill form fields (labels here are my own).
  • In the light theme the stage chips use dark text with a coloured dot, because the app's own chip colours are too pale to read comfortably.
  • Always uses the Chemlali window, and the parcel is an example.

Fifty olives. Four colours. One answer.

  1. Count

    Take a sample of about fifty olives from your parcel. Tap Green, Turning, Purple or Black for each one.

  2. Read

    The gauge says Too Early, Soon, Pick Now or Getting Late, set for your variety. Twelve varieties are built in.

  3. Log

    Record the kilos picked each day. Before pressing, you see an oil estimate as a range.

  4. Compare

    When the mill returns your oil, enter it and see the yield against last year and against your own estimate.

What the mill gave you, in one number.

Enter the olives you delivered and the oil you received. Zitouna shows the extraction rendement, then lines it up against last year and your own estimate.

Before you download.

Download

Android only. Like it? Support Zitouna.

Do I need an account?

No. Zitouna opens straight to your parcels and there is no sign-in.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Parcels, ripeness checks, harvest and mill logs, and the year view all work with no connection.

Which phones does it run on?

Android 8 or newer. There is no iPhone version.

How accurate is the gauge?

It is guidance, not a lab test. It points the way for your variety, so combine it with what you see in the grove.

Where is my data?

On your phone. A backup is a file you choose to send, for example by WhatsApp or email.
